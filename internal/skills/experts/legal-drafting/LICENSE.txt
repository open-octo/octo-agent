This skill is adapted from "legal-assistant-pro" in
lawyerwangbo/legal-assistant-pro

Sources within that repository:

- SKILL.md: the 案由匹配方法论 summary, the stage/party-role → document-type
  table, the general formatting convention, and the per-document drafting
  rules and "不作为" prohibitions (from the "诉讼文书起草规范" and "非诉分支"
  sections).
- docs/cause-of-action-reference.md: the cause-of-action category list and
  the four-step matching methodology, and its "常见错误" examples.
- templates/civil-complaint.md, templates/civil-answer.md,
  templates/appeal.md (反诉状/上诉状/再审申请书/强制执行申请书/代理词/财产
  保全申请书), templates/demand-letter.md (律师函/法律意见书/商事仲裁申请书),
  and the contract-drafting template found in this repository's templates
  directory (合同起草16项标准结构 and core-clause drafting points): the
  document skeletons, field placeholders, and drafting-point commentary.

Modifications for octo: consolidated the above files into a single skill,
added cross-references to octo's own `contract-review`, `legal-search`, and
`legal-reasoning` skills, added an explicit instruction to verify any cited
statute article number via `legal-search` before relying on it (the source
states several article numbers as bare facts without a verification
caveat), and removed the source's litigation-intake, evidence, labor-law,
and IP practice modules (out of scope for a drafting-only skill).
